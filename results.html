Reading the shape of the curve

A porous panel almost always shows a rising curve — little loss at 125 Hz, much more at 4 kHz. That is physics, not a mistake: a 125 Hz wave is nearly three metres long and simply pushes a thin light board out of its way, while a 4 kHz wave is eight centimetres long and gets tangled in the fibres.

If your curve is flat and low across every band, suspect a leak — sound going around the panel rather than through it.

Turning this into a conclusion

  • Quote the mean insertion loss and the range: "12 dB on average, from 4 dB at 125 Hz to 21 dB at 4 kHz."
  • Compare against the control panel, not against nothing.
  • Translate one number into human terms — "the 1 kHz tone was half as loud behind the bagasse board."
  • State the flanking limitation before a judge asks about it.

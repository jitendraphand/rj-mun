Sample details

Calibration

Optional. Insertion loss is a difference and stays correct without it — this only makes the absolute dB SPL numbers meaningful.

Hold a calibrated sound-level meter (or a school-lab decibel meter) beside this device, type what it reads, and tap Match now.

How the tone is recognised

The meter runs an 8192-point FFT on the microphone signal, finds the strongest peak, and checks whether it lands within 9 % of one of the six test frequencies. If it holds there for a third of a second, recording for that band begins.

Why the band level is different

The large number is the overall level of everything the microphone hears. The band level passes the signal through a band-pass filter centred on the detected tone first, so a fan, a fly and a conversation in the corridor stop contaminating the reading.

Everything stays on this device

Audio is analysed in the browser and never recorded, stored or transmitted. Readings are kept in this browser's local storage so they survive a refresh — and are erased when you start a new sample.
